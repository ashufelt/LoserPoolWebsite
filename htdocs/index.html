The rules

  • Pick a team you think will lose. Get it right and you survive the week.
  • No repeat picks — once you have used a team, it is gone for the season.
  • A tie eliminates you. A team that ties did not lose.
  • No teams playing Wednesday, Thursday or Friday. Those games start before the deadline. Saturday games are fine.
  • Teams on a bye cannot be picked.
  • Picks are open Tuesday to Saturday and lock Sunday and Monday. Everyone else's picks stay hidden until they lock.
  • Buy back in — week 1 only.
  • Final four or fewer can split the pot, if everyone still in agrees.

The dropdown only offers teams you are allowed to pick, so anything ineligible is already filtered out.
